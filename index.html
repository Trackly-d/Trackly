Google Calendar reminders, explained

Connecting Google Calendar is your choice. Here is exactly what Trackly does with it.

What Trackly does

  • Adds reminder events for your assignments to your main Google Calendar.
  • Removes them when you mark an assignment Submitted, delete it, or switch reminders off.
  • Works only with the events it created itself.

What Trackly does not do

  • Read or change your other calendar events.
  • Access your email or your contacts.
  • Sell your Google data or use it for advertising.

You can switch Google Calendar reminders off in Profile at any time. Reminders are Google Calendar popups, so they follow your own Calendar notification settings. Read exactly how we handle Google data.

Trackly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions and answers

What is Trackly?

Trackly is a student assignment tracker. You add your courses and assignments with due dates, follow their status, see them on a month calendar and, if you want, get reminders in Google Calendar.

Do I need Google Calendar?

No. You can use Trackly without it. Connecting Google Calendar is optional and is only needed for reminders.

How do the reminders work?

With the default schedule, Trackly adds a reminder each morning at 8:00, starting the day after you add the assignment, until the day before it is due. On the due day you get alerts 2 hours, 1 hour and 30 minutes before, and at the due time. You can also choose your own reminder times. Reminders are Google Calendar popups, so they follow your own Calendar notification settings and are not a guarantee.

What happens to my reminders when I submit?

When you mark an assignment Submitted, delete it, or switch Google Calendar reminders off, Trackly removes the reminders it added.

Who can use Trackly?

Trackly is meant for students aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account yourself in Settings. Or email us through our contact page, and we will delete your account and your data within 30 days.
