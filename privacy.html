Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Trackly collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and the choices you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We do not sell your personal information.

In short

  • We collect your name, email, courses and assignments so Trackly can work for you.
  • We never sell your information and we do not show ads.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, Trackly only adds and removes its own reminder events. Details are in section 4.
  • You can switch Google Calendar and push notifications off at any time, and delete your account yourself in Settings.
  • Service providers (Google Firebase, Cloudinary, Cloudflare and Pixabay) help us run Trackly, as explained in section 5.
  • You have rights over your information, including access, correction, deletion and objection. They are explained in section 9.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Trackly is run by a team of six people in Nigeria. Trackly is not yet a registered company. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the Trackly team is the "data controller" of your information, which means we decide how and why it is used.

For privacy and data protection questions or requests, email trackly409@gmail.com. We have not named a separate Data Protection Officer, so this address reaches the people who handle your information. If the law requires us to register with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, we will do so.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so we cannot see it.
  • What you add: your courses (title, code, instructor, colour and photo), your assignments (title, assignment question or notes, due date, priority, status and reminder choices), and a profile photo if you upload one.
  • Sign up record: the date you created your account, and the date and version of the terms you agreed to.
  • Alerts and history: your alert choices in Settings, a short history of events in your account (such as an assignment or course being added, started, edited or marked as submitted), and which notifications you have read.
  • Push notifications, only if you switch them on in Settings: a push address for each device you allow (it lets your browser's push service reach that device), which kinds of push you chose, and, for assignments that are still open, the title, course name and due time, so Trackly can send due soon and overdue alerts while it is closed.
  • Google Calendar details, only if you connect it: see section 4.
  • Basic technical data: information such as device and browser type, which our service providers may collect to keep Trackly working and secure.

3. How we use your information

  • to create your account and keep you signed in;
  • to show your courses and assignments;
  • to show alerts and a short history in Trackly, such as when an assignment is due soon or overdue;
  • to send push notifications to your devices, if you switch them on;
  • to add and remove reminder events on your Google Calendar, if you connect it;
  • to find a suitable photo for your course header;
  • to keep Trackly secure, fix problems and improve it;
  • to answer your questions and meet our legal duties.

We collect only the information Trackly needs to work, and we use it only for the reasons listed above. We do not use your information to make automated decisions that significantly affect you. Statuses such as Overdue are worked out from the due dates you enter, and they only change what you see inside Trackly.

4. Google user data and Google Calendar

This section explains exactly how Trackly uses Google user data. It only applies if you choose to connect Google Calendar. Signing in with Google is separate: it only gives us your name, email address and profile photo.

What Trackly asks for

When you switch on Google Calendar Reminders in your Profile, Google asks you to allow Trackly to use the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned. This permission lets an app view, create, change and delete events on calendars you own. It is the only Google Calendar permission we ask for, and we only use the part of it described below.

What Trackly does with it

  • Creates reminder events on your main Google Calendar for your assignments: a "Due:" event at the due time with popup reminders, and, if you use the default schedule, a repeating "Work on:" event each morning at 8:00 until the day before the due date. Each event contains the assignment title, the course name, the due time and a link back to the assignment in Trackly.
  • Removes those events when they are no longer needed: when you mark the assignment Submitted, delete it, change something shown in the event, or switch Google Calendar Reminders off.
  • Finds its own events: every event Trackly creates carries a private tag. When you switch the feature off, Trackly searches for events with that tag, and before deleting an event it checks the tag. Trackly does not read, copy, change or delete any other event in your calendar, and it does not access your email, contacts or any other Google data.

What we store, and where

  • A long-lived Google access code (a "refresh token") that lets Trackly keep working after you close the page. It is encrypted and kept in our Cloudflare storage, linked only to your account. It is never stored in your browser or in our database.
  • A short-lived Google access token, kept only in the memory of our server for a few minutes while it is used. It is not saved.
  • In our Firebase database: the IDs of the calendar events Trackly created and your time zone (saved with each assignment), a note that Google Calendar is connected, and, briefly, a list of event ids waiting to be removed if Google could not be reached.
  • The events themselves live in your Google Calendar, which Google controls.

What we never do

  • We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not share it with anyone except the providers that run Trackly for us (section 5).
  • We do not allow people to read it unless you ask us to, it is needed for security or support, or the law requires it.
  • We do not use it to develop or train artificial intelligence models.

How to stop

Switch off Google Calendar Reminders in your Profile. Trackly removes the reminders it created, takes back its permission from Google and deletes the stored token. You can also remove Trackly's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party access settings. If you do, Trackly will notice, delete the stored token and ask you to connect again. Reminders that were already in your calendar at that point can be deleted by you in Google Calendar: look for events starting with "Due:" or "Work on:".

Trackly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Trackly, and only as much as they need:

  • Google (Firebase): sign in, our database and hosting, and Google Calendar if you connect it.
  • Cloudinary: storing and delivering images, including photos you upload.
  • Cloudflare: running the services that connect Trackly to Google Calendar, to photo search and to push notifications, and storing your encrypted Google Calendar token and your push settings.
  • Your browser's push service (run by Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla, depending on your browser): if you switch push on, it delivers each notification to your device. It receives your device's push address and the notification, which is encrypted so that only your device can read it.
  • Pixabay: course photos. We send only the subject words of a course, never your name or email.

We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of Trackly or its users. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Why we are allowed to use your information

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, together with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission's General Application and Implementation Directive 2025, says we must have a lawful reason to use your information. We rely on:

  • Your consent, which you give when you create an account and agree to our terms, and when you choose to connect Google Calendar or switch on push notifications;
  • What we need to provide the service you asked for, such as saving your courses and assignments and showing you alerts;
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping Trackly safe and working, which never overrides your rights;
  • Our legal duties, where the law requires us to keep or share certain information.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. For example, you can switch off Google Calendar Reminders in Profile, switch off push notifications in Settings, or delete your account in Settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew it, but we will stop using your information for that purpose.

7. Where your information is kept and how we protect it

Where it is kept

Your information is stored on the servers of our service providers (Google, Cloudflare and Cloudinary), which may be outside Nigeria. When your information is handled outside Nigeria, it goes only to the providers named in section 5. We choose providers that publish their own data protection commitments and use industry standard security, and we do not send your information to anyone else abroad.

How we protect it

  • Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so we cannot see it.
  • Our database rules let each signed in student read and write only their own records, never another student's.
  • Your Google Calendar token is encrypted, and it is never stored in your browser or in our database.
  • Push notifications are encrypted from our server to your device, so the push service that delivers them cannot read them.
  • Information moves between your device and Trackly over secure (HTTPS) connections.

No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe and we review how we protect it when we change Trackly.

If something goes wrong

If a breach of personal data happens, we will find out what was affected and act to contain it. Where the law requires, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. If the breach is likely to put you at high risk, we will also tell you without undue delay, in clear language, and explain what you can do to protect yourself.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account, courses and assignments: while your account is active.
  • Your Google Calendar token: until you switch Google Calendar Reminders off, remove Trackly in your Google Account, or ask us to delete your account.
  • Calendar event ids waiting to be removed: deleted as soon as the events are removed.
  • Your notification history: for 30 days, then it is removed.
  • Push notification data: your device's push address until you switch push off, log out on that device, or delete your account. The title, course name and due time of an open assignment are kept only until it is submitted, deleted or past due, or you switch push off.
  • Photos you upload: while your account is active. We remove them when your account is deleted, and when you remove or replace a photo.
  • When you delete your account in Settings, Trackly removes your Google Calendar reminders, your push notification data, your photos, your courses, assignments and notification history, and your login, straight away.
  • If you ask us by email instead, we delete your personal information within 30 days, unless we must keep some of it for legal reasons.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you, and tell you how it is used;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information or close your account (you can do this yourself with Delete account in Settings);
  • limit how we use your information;
  • stop using your information for a purpose you object to;
  • give you a copy of your information in a common format, so you can move it elsewhere.

You can also withdraw your consent at any time (see section 6). You have the right not to be subject to decisions made only by automated means that significantly affect you, and Trackly does not make such decisions.

Email trackly409@gmail.com and we will respond to your request within 30 days. We may first need to check that the request really comes from you, so please write from the address on your account. To delete your account, use Delete account in Settings, or email us from the address on your account.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

10. Cookies and browser storage

Trackly uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in (for longer if you choose "Remember me"), to keep a short-lived copy of recent screens so pages load faster (it clears when you close the tab), and to remember a few small settings, such as which list filter you chose. If you switch on push or install Trackly, your browser also keeps a small background program for Trackly (a service worker) and a push subscription. The service worker keeps only an offline page, never your courses or assignments. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools.

11. Children

Trackly is meant for students aged 18 and over. Secondary school students under 18 may use Trackly only with permission from a parent or guardian. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian before you create an account.

We take extra care with the information of children. We collect only what Trackly needs to work, we never use it for advertising, and we never sell it. If we learn that we hold information about a child without that permission, we will delete it. A parent or guardian can email us to see, correct or delete their child's information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email. If we ever want to use Google user data in a new way, we will update this policy and ask for your permission first.
