Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Trackly collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Trackly is run by [Trackly team or company name]. For privacy questions or requests, email [support email, for example support@trackly.ng].

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so we cannot see it.
  • What you add: your courses (title, code, instructor, colour and photo), assignments, due dates and reminder settings.
  • Sign up record: the date you created your account and the date you agreed to our terms.
  • Basic technical data: information such as device and browser type, which our service providers may collect to keep Trackly working and secure.

3. How we use your information

  • to create your account and keep you signed in;
  • to show your courses and assignments and send the reminders you ask for;
  • to find a suitable photo for your course header;
  • to keep Trackly secure, fix problems and improve it;
  • to answer your questions and meet our legal duties.

4. Google user data and Google Calendar

If you connect your Google Calendar, Trackly asks for your permission first. We use that access only to create and manage the reminder events you ask for, and for no other purpose. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it. We do not allow people to read your Google data unless you give permission, it is needed for security or support, or the law requires it.

Trackly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Trackly's access at any time in your Google account's security settings.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Trackly, and only as much as they need:

  • Google (Firebase): sign in, our database and hosting.
  • Cloudinary: delivering and storing images so they load quickly.
  • Cloudflare: running the services that connect Trackly to other tools.
  • Pexels: course photos. We send only the subject words of a course, never your name or email.

We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of Trackly or its users.

6. Why we are allowed to use your information

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we rely on your consent (when you create an account and agree to our terms), on what we need to provide the service you asked for, and on our legitimate interest in keeping Trackly safe and working.

7. Where your information is kept

Your information is stored on the servers of our service providers, which may be outside Nigeria. We choose providers that use industry standard security, and we take steps to protect your information wherever it is kept.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information, unless we must keep some of it for legal reasons.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information or close your account;
  • stop or limit how we use your information, or give you a copy of it.

Email [support email] and we will respond as soon as we can. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

10. Cookies and browser storage

Trackly uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in (if you choose "Remember me") and to remember small settings so the app loads faster. We do not use advertising cookies.

11. Children

Trackly is meant for students aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian. If we learn that we hold information about a child without that permission, we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email.

13. Contact us

Email [support email].
